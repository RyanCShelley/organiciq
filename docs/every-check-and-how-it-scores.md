# Every check, and how it scores

Generated by `python -m app.rules_catalog`. The lever, what it spends,
whether it preempts, and the confidence, urgency and effort are read out
of the code; what each rule checks is maintained beside it and audited
with `--audit`.

**Growth actions are a limited budget.** Core work is reported and never
spends one. Blocking checks preempt within their page.

## The scoring formula

```
relevance = min(1, impact / score_impact_relevance_scale)
score = w_impact x impact
      + (w_confidence x confidence + w_urgency x urgency + w_effort x (100 - effort))
        x relevance
```

Weights: impact 0.6, confidence 0.15, urgency 0.15, ease 0.1; relevance scale 20. All five are per-client rows.

Impact is always leads: `(leads / (0.25 x period goal)) x 100`, capped at 100,
then discounted for how the number was arrived at (measured 1.0, inferred
0.85, estimated 0.55). On a 20-lead goal, impact 25 is 1.25 leads.

Promoted at impact >= 25 and confidence >= 50. A blocking technical fault clears a
lower bar of 5 — lower, not absent.

### `tracking` — Are conversions being recorded at all?

**Fires when:** No conversions in 14 days while sessions still arrive, and the fortnight should have produced at least 3 leads (or, with no history, 500 sessions arrived).

**Impact:** Leads the fortnight should have produced, as leads at risk.

**Note:** The only check that suppresses others: every score below it is computed from a lead count it says is wrong.

### `tracking_partial` — Has one page stopped converting while the rest of the site has not?

**Fires when:** A page produces nothing for `partial_break_days` (14) days while the site still converts, and its own prior rate applied to the traffic it still gets expects at least `partial_break_min_expected_leads` (3) leads.

**Impact:** Expected leads from that page, as leads at risk.

**Note:** Suppresses nothing. A page with no visitors is skipped: no leads is arithmetic, not a fault.

### `tracking_spike` — Are leads far above the usual rate?

**Fires when:** Leads past `lead_spike_multiple` (3.0)x the rate the same traffic used to produce, with at least `lead_spike_min_leads` (10) recorded.

**Impact:** The excess over the expected rate — the recorded leads that may not exist.

**Note:** What double firing and form spam look like.

### `site_conversion` — Is the site converting at the level the plan requires?

**Fires when:** The lead rate fell `conversion_lead_rate_decline_min_pct` (10.0)%, or sessions grew `conversion_sessions_growth_min_pct` (5.0)% without leads following, or the rate sits below the client's own starting baseline. Each trigger needs `gate1_min_expected_leads` (10) expected leads behind it.

**Impact:** Leads lost against the previous period, or the gap to plan.

**Note:** Routes to one page when 60% of the loss sits there; says 'hold the plan' when last year fell the same way.

### `conversion_page` — Does a page earn traffic and convert below its own page type?

**Fires when:** The page type has at least `gate3_page_type_min_pages` (5) pages and `gate3_page_type_min_leads` (10) leads behind it to compare against.

**Impact:** Leads the page would produce at its page type's rate.

### `no_conversion_element` — Is there anything on the page to convert through?

**Fires when:** No form, phone link, email link or call-to-action button on a page with `cta_min_sessions` (30) sessions. Only counted from a page that returned 2xx.

**Impact:** Scored on the page's own traffic.

**Note:** A null count means the crawl predates the check, which is not the same as zero.

## Internal Linking & Site Architecture

| Check | Spends | Preempts | Conf | Urg | Effort | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `internal_linking` | growth action |  | 75 | 55 | 30 | S |

### `internal_linking` — Do enough pages link to it?

**Fires when:** Editorial inbound links below the floor for what the page is for — `link_floor_money` (10) for money pages, `link_floor_industry` (6) for industry pages, `link_floor_blog` (3) for posts — at position 4 to 20.

**Impact:** Clicks a better position would earn, converted at the site's lead rate.

**Note:** The homepage is never reported. Donors capped at `link_max_donors` (3).

## SERP & CTR Optimization

| `rank_push` | growth action |  | 80 | 50 | 20 | S |
| `title_missing` | growth action |  | 80 | 50 | 20 | S |
| `title_duplicate` | growth action |  | 80 | 50 | 20 | S |

### `serp_ctr` — Is the listing earning the clicks the position should?

**Fires when:** Position 1 to `serp_ctr_max_position` (5), at least 1,000 impressions, CTR under half the measured curve, and at least 5 clicks recoverable. Pages where brand is over half the impressions are skipped.

**Impact:** Recoverable clicks converted at the site's lead rate.

**Note:** Stops at five on purpose: the curve pays 0.73% at six, so there is no click to win back by rewriting a listing.

### `rank_push` — Does a page sit just below where the clicks are?

**Fires when:** Position above `serp_ctr_max_position` (5) and up to `rank_push_max_position` (10), with at least `rank_push_min_impressions` (200) impressions and 5 clicks to gain at position five.

**Impact:** Clicks gained reaching position five, converted at the site's lead rate.

**Note:** The band CTR work cannot reach. The instruction is rank, not the listing.

### `title_missing` — Does the page have a title?

**Fires when:** Empty title on a page with demand.

**Impact:** As above.

**Note:** Files under SERP & CTR, not Technical: a title earns the click, so it competes for an action.

### `title_duplicate` — Does the title belong to another page too?

**Fires when:** Duplicate title on a page with demand.

**Impact:** As above.

**Note:** Also SERP & CTR.

## Search & AI Visibility

| Check | Spends | Preempts | Conf | Urg | Effort | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `keyword_not_ranking` | growth action |  | 75 | 60 | 40 | L |
| `keyword_fell_top5` | growth action |  | 75 | 60 | 40 | L |
| `keyword_fell_top10` | growth action |  | 75 | 60 | 40 | L |
| `prompt_not_cited` | growth action |  | 75 | 60 | 40 | L |
| `ai_crawlers_blocked` | growth action |  | 75 | 60 | 40 | L |

### `keyword_not_ranking` — Is a tracked term outside the top 100?

**Fires when:** Volume at least `ai_visibility_min_keyword_volume` (50); the top `ai_visibility_keyword_top_n` (25) by volume are reported.

**Impact:** Volume x the curve at `keyword_target_position` (3) x a difficulty discount (floored at `keyword_difficulty_floor` (0.2)) x the site's lead rate, capped at `single_opportunity_max_lead_share` (0.5) of the period goal.

**Note:** Reads the keyword-to-page map first; without one it asks for the mapping rather than guessing.

### `keyword_fell_top5` — Did a term fall out of the top five?

**Fires when:** Previous position 5 or better, now worse than 5.

**Impact:** 18% of volume as recoverable clicks, converted at the site's lead rate.

**Note:** A term that was ranking has shown it can rank, so the share is a property of the fall rather than the curve.

### `keyword_fell_top10` — Did a term fall out of the top ten?

**Fires when:** Previous position 10 or better, now worse than 10.

**Impact:** 10% of volume as recoverable clicks, converted at the site's lead rate.

### `prompt_not_cited` — Do answer engines cite the brand for a tracked prompt?

**Fires when:** At least `ai_visibility_prompt_min_checks` (2) checks in the period with no citation; the top `ai_visibility_prompt_top_n` (25) are reported.

**Impact:** 12% of prompt volume as recoverable clicks, converted at the site's lead rate.

**Note:** A blocked AI crawler preempts everything else: an engine that cannot fetch the page will never cite it.

### `ai_crawlers_blocked` — Can answer engines fetch the site?

**Fires when:** robots.txt disallows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended or similar.

**Impact:** Reported through the prompt rule rather than scored on its own.

**Note:** Usually a plugin's default rule rather than a decision anyone made.

### `status_error` — Does the page return 4xx or 5xx?

**Fires when:** On a page with demand, or in the sitemap, or commercial/conversion.

**Impact:** Addressable clicks behind the error, converted at the site's lead rate.

### `soft_404` — Does the page return 200 while saying it is missing?

**Fires when:** A 2xx whose title or H1 is about being not found.

**Impact:** As above.

**Note:** Google drops these exactly as it drops a real 404. SE Ranking has no such check; the first-party crawler does it.

### `non_indexable` — Is the page excluded from the index?

**Fires when:** noindex, on a page with demand. A working redirect is not reported.

**Impact:** As above.

### `canonical_elsewhere` — Does the canonical point somewhere unusable?

**Fires when:** The target is off-site, missing from the crawl, erroring, or itself non-indexable.

**Impact:** As above.

**Note:** A canonical pointing at a live page on the same site is correct consolidation and is not reported.

### `orphan_page` — Does anything link to the page?

**Fires when:** No inbound internal links, with at least `orphan_min_impressions` (30) impressions.

**Impact:** As above.

**Note:** Demand is the qualifier: most orphans are drafts and thank-you pages.

### `blocked_resources` — Can Google render the page?

**Fires when:** Scripts or stylesheets the page loads that robots.txt disallows, on the same host.

**Impact:** As above.

**Note:** A CDN has its own rules we have not read, so its resources are not counted.

### `broken_redirect` — Does a redirect land on an error?

**Fires when:** A 3xx whose target returns 4xx or 5xx.

**Impact:** As above.

### `redirect_chain` — How many hops before the page?

**Fires when:** Three or more.

**Impact:** As above.

### `description_missing` — Does the page have a meta description?

**Fires when:** Empty description on a page with demand.

**Impact:** As above.

**Note:** Core work. Google rewrites descriptions at will.

### `description_duplicate` — Does the description belong to another page too?

**Fires when:** Duplicate description on a page with demand.

**Impact:** As above.

**Note:** Core work.

### `missing_schema` — Does the page carry structured data that says what it is?

**Fires when:** No markup, or only the site-wide boilerplate a plugin emits everywhere.

**Impact:** As above.

**Note:** Core work. Only on pages the first-party crawl actually reached.

### `invalid_schema` — Does the structured data parse?

**Fires when:** Markup present and unreadable.

**Impact:** As above.

**Note:** Core work.

### `sitemap_missing` — Is there an XML sitemap?

**Fires when:** None declared and none at the usual locations, on any host.

**Impact:** Site-level; scored on the site's own figures.

**Note:** Core work.

### `robots_disallow_crawling` — Does robots.txt disallow crawling?

**Fires when:** robots.txt tells our agent not to crawl the site at all.

**Impact:** Site-level; reaches the engine as the blocking signal `robots_blocking`.

**Note:** Blocking: it stops everything else being measurable.

### `no_robots` — Is there a robots.txt?

**Fires when:** Nothing served at /robots.txt, or what is served is not a robots file.

**Impact:** Site-level.

**Note:** Core work. Advisory — a missing robots.txt means crawl freely.

### `robots_not_accessible` — Can robots.txt be fetched?

**Fires when:** The request for it failed, as opposed to returning nothing.

**Impact:** Site-level.

**Note:** Core work. Different from absent: something is there and unreachable.

### `robots_has_errors` — Is the declared sitemap readable?

**Fires when:** robots.txt declares a sitemap and it yields nothing.

**Impact:** Site-level.

**Note:** Core work. Declared and broken is worse than absent: something references it.

## Causes a finding can report

| Cause | Means |
|---|---|
| `analytics_tag_broken` | The analytics tag stopped firing site-wide |
| `conversion_event_broken` | Sessions are normal and the lead event is not firing |
| `form_broken` | The form itself is not submitting |
| `partial_tracking_break` | One form or template stopped while the rest convert |
| `sessions_fell` | The page lost traffic, so the drop is search, not the page |
| `traffic_mix_shifted` | The same page is getting lower-intent visitors |
| `mobile_regression` | Mobile converts far below desktop, or fell on its own |
| `page_changed` | The page itself changed |
| `speed_regression` | The page got materially slower |
| `drop_concentrated` | Most of the lost leads are on one page group |
| `drop_sitewide` | The drop is even across segments, so something global changed |
| `behind_plan` | Nothing fell; the site has never converted at the level the plan needs |
| `seasonal` | The same dip happened a year ago |
| `spam_filtered` | Lead count fell while qualified leads held |
| `ctr_loss` | Impressions held and clicks fell, so the listing lost the click |
| `true_decay` | Impressions and rankings both fell |
| `demand_fell` | Rankings held and search demand fell |
| `self_competition` | Another of our URLs took the queries |
| `no_page_for_term` | Nothing on the site targets the term |
| `page_cannot_rank` | A page exists and something stops it ranking |
| `page_not_competitive` | The page can rank and is being beaten |
| `ai_crawlers_blocked` | Answer engines are not allowed to fetch the site |
| `no_page_answers_prompt` | No page answers the question |
| `page_not_quotable` | A page answers it in a form an engine cannot quote |
| `rank_push` | It has the demand and sits below where clicks happen |
| `conversion_proof_missing` | Visitors leave before the page says anything |
| `conversion_cta_missing` | Nothing in the page's content asks for the next step |
| `conversion_offer_missing` | No page is declared as where a lead goes |
| `ai_readiness_gaps` | Structured data misidentifies or under-describes the brand |
| `topic_no_hub` | A cluster of pages has nothing tying it together |
| `no_asset_for_query` | People search for a tool the site does not have |
| `under_linked_donors_found` | Pages that share its subject do not link to it |
| `under_linked_no_donor` | Nothing on the site shares its subject |
| `undetermined` | The checks did not settle on a cause |
